**IBM - Business Trends 2026: Leading in the Intelligence Age: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. 81% of employees confident they can keep up with AI**: Four in five employees believe they can keep pace with future technology advances. But 61% expect their job to change significantly in 2026 due to AI—and half fear obsolescence by 2030.

**2. 63% open to working with AI agents—half willing to be managed by one**: Nearly two-thirds of employees are open to working with AI agents, and almost half would accept being managed by one. The resistance narrative is overstated.

**3. More than half would switch companies to learn AI—42% would take a pay cut**: Over half of employees would change employers to learn AI skills; 42% would accept lower pay for the opportunity. The hunger for AI training is intense.

**4. 93% of executives factor AI sovereignty into 2026 strategy**: Nearly all executives say AI sovereignty—control over AI systems, data, and infrastructure—must be part of their 2026 strategy. Geopolitics meets machine learning.

**5. 2026 is the year of operating AI agents at scale**: Enterprises will run dozens or even hundreds of agents in production, built by multiple teams across multiple platforms. The agentic pivot defines 2026.

**6. 56% believe workforce needs retraining due to AI automation**: Over half of executives expect their workforce will need new training because of AI-driven automation. Leaders should anticipate at least half their people need reskilling by year-end.

**7. 74% say volatility creates new business opportunities**: Three-quarters of executives see economic and geopolitical volatility creating new opportunities. Only a third are optimistic about the global economy, but four in five are confident in their own organization.

**8. Quantum advantage likely by end of 2026**: IBM research indicates quantum advantage—quantum computers outperforming classical methods—likely emerges by late 2026. Quantum-ready organizations are 3x more likely to belong to multiple ecosystems.

**9. Smaller, domain-tuned reasoning models will dominate**: "We're going to see smaller reasoning models that are multimodal and easier to tune for specific domains." Open-source AI appetite grows as enterprises seek efficiency.

**10. 1,000+ C-suite and 8,500 global respondents surveyed**: IBM IBV surveyed over 1,000 C-suite executives and 8,500 global consumers and employees. The verdict: adapt to the Intelligence Age or be left behind.
