**Capgemini - AI Perspectives 2026: The Multi-Year AI Advantage: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. AI budget allocation jumps from 3% to 5% of business budgets**: Organizations expect to allocate 5% of annual business budgets to AI by 2026, up from 3% in 2025. The investment is scaling with the ambition.

**2. 66% report measurable productivity gains from human-AI collaboration**: Two-thirds of organizations report measurable improvements in productivity and decision quality through human-AI collaboration. AI is amplifying—not replacing—human capabilities.

**3. Six in 10 redefining skillsets and investing in upskilling**: 60% are actively redefining skillsets and investing in workforce upskilling. The organizations preparing their people are the ones capturing value.

**4. 54% prioritizing data sovereignty and control**: More than half prioritize data control as AI sovereignty concerns mount. Organizations balance build-versus-buy to combine speed with differentiation.

**5. Nearly two-thirds pausing low-value AI projects**: Organizations are redirecting resources from scattered experiments toward high-impact areas. The spray-and-pray era is ending; focused execution is beginning.

**6. Shift from experimentation to long-term value creation**: Organizations are accelerating investments prioritizing functions with well-defined processes and measurable outcomes. Experimentation gave way to transformation.

**7. More than half of CXOs using AI for strategic decisions**: Over half of CXOs actively use AI to support or inform strategic decision-making—expected to more than double within three years. Another third are experimenting.

**8. Governance, skills, and accountability are the new priorities**: Leaders must be more deliberate about governance, skills, accountability, and human-AI chemistry to realize transformative value. The soft stuff is the hard stuff.

**9. From hype to realism—pragmatic enterprise-wide transformation**: "We have now entered a new, more pragmatic era of AI-driven transformation, focused on longer-term, enterprise-wide implementations." —Pascal Brier, Chief Innovation Officer

**10. Infrastructure, data, and workforce are the foundation**: Organizations focus on infrastructure, data, governance, and workforce upskilling, laying strong foundation for AI adoption and impact. Build the base or build on sand.
