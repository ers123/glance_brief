**JPMorgan - 2026 Market Outlook: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. Three forces drive 2026: monetary policy, AI supercycle, market polarization**: JPMorgan identifies three powerful forces shaping markets—uneven monetary policy, the unstoppable AI supercycle, and deepening polarization across markets and economies. The trifecta defines the year.

**2. AI supercycle is "the real game changer"**: The AI supercycle drives record CapEx levels and rapid earnings growth, especially in US equities. AI isn't just a tech story anymore—it's spreading into banks, healthcare, logistics, and utilities.

**3. S&P 500 earnings growth of 13-15% for at least two years**: JPMorgan Research estimates the AI supercycle will drive above-trend S&P 500 earnings growth of 13-15% for at least the next two years. The earnings machine runs on AI.

**4. Double-digit equity gains forecast across DM and EM**: JPMorgan is positive on global equities, forecasting double-digit gains across both developed and emerging markets in 2026. Bulls have reason to run.

**5. $160-191B fiscal injection hitting 110M+ taxpayers**: Massive fiscal liquidity injection—$160-191 billion—flowing directly to over 110 million taxpayers in coming months. Estimated to add 0.8% to US Real GDP in Q1 alone.

**6. 35% recession risk, but soft landing possible**: JPMorgan cites 35% recession probability with scenarios including soft landing extension if AI productivity outpaces inflation—or mid-year stall if tariff costs eat margins.

**7. Credit issuance pickup driven by AI ecosystem**: In credit markets, JPMorgan expects notable pickup in net issuance, particularly from the AI ecosystem and AI-adjacent companies. The credit cycle follows the AI cycle.

**8. AI spreading beyond tech into every sector**: AI's impact extends into banks, healthcare, logistics, and utilities. The theme is no longer sector-specific—it's economy-wide.

**9. Fiscal fuel and AI peak converging**: The "Great 2026 Rebound" narrative combines fiscal stimulus with AI supercycle convergence. Both forces amplify each other.

**10. Markets reward AI productivity, punish inflation**: Soft landing scenario depends on AI productivity outpacing inflationary pressures. If AI delivers, the reward is double-digit gains. If it doesn't, margins compress.
