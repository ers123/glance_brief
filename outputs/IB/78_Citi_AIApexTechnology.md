**Citi - AI: The Information Era's Apex Technology: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. AI revenues to grow 80% annually to $780B by 2030**: According to Citi's AI Industry Model, AI revenues will grow nearly 80% annually over five years to reach $780 billion in 2030 from $43 billion today. The growth rate defies normal business physics.

**2. Banking profits to $2 trillion by 2028—$170B AI boost**: Citi GPS projects AI adds $170 billion to global banking profits, reaching $2 trillion by 2028. Banks' AI-related tech spend hits $40 billion by 2026. Finance cashes in first.

**3. 54% of banking jobs face high automation potential**: Citi found that 54% of jobs in banking have higher potential for automation, while another 12% could be augmented by AI. Two-thirds of banking roles will transform.

**4. 37% of VC funding went to AI startups in 2024**: More than a third of venture capital funding was directed to AI startups in 2024—and that percentage is likely higher in 2025. The capital allocation shift is historic.

**5. Autonomous agents saw biggest growth in VC deal activity**: Autonomous agents and digital co-workers saw the largest growth in VC deal activity in 2024, followed by GenAI for customer support. The smart money is on agents.

**6. "AI may commoditize human intelligence"**: Citi notes that "just as the steam engine powered the industrial revolution, and the internet ushered in the age of information, AI may commoditize human intelligence." The analogy isn't hyperbole.

**7. Agentic AI turbocharges the "Do It For Me" economy**: Citi observes that "Agentic AI effectively turbocharges the Do It For Me economy." Users will have AI agents helping them choose products and execute transactions.

**8. Tech stack being fundamentally remade**: The report explores how AI is remaking every layer of the technology stack—from infrastructure to applications. Nothing stays untouched.

**9. Private companies play critical role in AI advancement**: Citi's analysis highlights the venture-capital ecosystem and critical role of private companies in AI development. The innovation engine is private, not public.

**10. Finance: data-rich and at the forefront of change**: Finance—being data-rich with clients adopting AI at pace—will be at the forefront of AI-driven transformation. The industry writes the playbook others will follow.
