**BCG - How Agentic AI Is Transforming Enterprise Platforms: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. 25-40% reduction in low-value work time**: AI agents cut employees' low-value work time by 25-40%—and even more in some cases. These agents work 24/7, handle data spikes without headcount increases, and never complain about overtime.

**2. 30-50% process acceleration across functions**: AI-powered workflows accelerate business processes 30-50% in finance, procurement, and customer operations. Early adopters see 20-30% faster workflow cycles. Speed compounds into competitive advantage.

**3. ServiceNow agents cut manual workloads 60%**: Real-world deployment: ServiceNow AI agents reduce manual workloads by up to 60%. Auto-resolving IT tickets, rerouting supplies for inventory shortages, triggering procurement flows—all without human input.

**4. Insurance claims handled 40% faster**: AI agents handling insurance claims end-to-end—document validation, triage, escalation, payout—cut handling time by 40% and lift NPS by 15 points. Customers and shareholders both win.

**5. 25% lift in lead conversion for B2B SaaS**: AI-driven campaign managers testing, adapting, and optimizing touchpoints in real time deliver 25% improvement in lead conversion. Marketing automation just leveled up.

**6. 60% reduction in risk events in pilot environments**: Autonomous anomaly detection, cash forecasting, and account reallocation cut risk events by 60% in finance pilots. The machines see patterns humans miss.

**7. Kill switches and spending caps are mandatory**: Guardrails, tool hardening, strict schemas, safe defaults, allow lists, input checks, timeouts, and spending caps—all essential. Red team drills and sandbox testing before production. Safety isn't optional.

**8. Legacy systems aren't the blocker you think**: AI serves as smart middleware, with LLM-powered connectors auto-generating APIs from old codebases and wrapping legacy workflows with AI-driven automation. Integration is solved; excuses are expired.

**9. AI talent is the real bottleneck**: Required roles: AI-prompt engineers, ML specialists, data engineers, business translators who map AI to workflows. Finding them is the constraint. Start hiring yesterday.

**10. AI-assisted isn't enough—AI-orchestrated is the future**: The endgame isn't AI helping humans. It's AI agents learning, adapting, and running enterprise processes in real time. Companies that embrace this decisively gain permanent edges in productivity, responsiveness, and innovation.
