**BCG - AI Is Raising the Stakes in Cybersecurity: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. More than half of execs rank AI cyber risks top three**: A global BCG survey of 500 senior leaders shows AI cyber risks among their top organizational concerns. Yet budgets, talent, technology maturity, and regulations aren't keeping pace. Recognition without action is theater.

**2. Offense scales faster than defense can modernize**: The uncomfortable truth: AI-powered attacks are accelerating at machine speed while organizational defenses remain largely reactive. The asymmetry is getting more dangerous, not less.

**3. $25 million lost to a single deepfake CFO video**: A multinational engineering firm lost $25 million after employees were deceived by an AI-generated deepfake impersonating the CFO. Telecom fined $1M for AI voice-cloned election robocalls. These attacks are happening now.

**4. 60% believe they've already been hit by AI attacks**: Six in ten leaders believe they've already encountered AI-enabled attacks. But only 7% have deployed AI-enabled defense. 88% plan to—eventually. The gap between belief and action is cavernous.

**5. Only 5% have increased cyber spending for AI threats**: Despite rising exposure, only 5% of companies have significantly increased cyber spending due to AI threats. Budgets are flat while attack surfaces expand. This math doesn't work.

**6. 70% can't hire AI-cyber talent**: Nearly 70% of organizations struggle to hire people with combined AI and cybersecurity expertise. The talent pool is tiny; competition is fierce. Build or buy—but do something.

**7. AI systems themselves become prime targets**: As organizations embed AI across products and operations, AI models, training data, prompting interfaces, and agentic tools emerge as new critical assets requiring protection. Your AI is now attack surface.

**8. Attacks become autonomous and adaptive**: AI has advanced from static models to agentic systems that observe, reason, and act—launching multistep operations without human oversight, adapting instantly to defensive signals, probing continuously for vulnerabilities.

**9. Identity-based authentication is failing**: Deepfakes, voice cloning, and synthetic identities have undermined traditional identity verification. Attackers replicate executives, employees, and customers with convincing precision. The old safeguards are broken.

**10. CEOs and CISOs must lead together**: Organizations need dual leadership—CEOs prioritizing cybersecurity at board level while CISOs accelerate high-impact AI-enabled defense deployment. The era of passive defense is over. Shape the landscape or be shaped by it.
