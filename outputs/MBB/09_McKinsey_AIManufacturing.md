**McKinsey - AI in Manufacturing: From Pilots to Performance: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. COOs have high hopes and high budgets—but gaps remain**: A survey of 100+ COOs at $1B+ manufacturers reveals ambitious AI goals and significant investments. But many underinvest in the enablers needed for AI to generate lasting value. Money without foundations is waste.

**2. The "lights out" factory is no longer fantasy**: The vision: factories so automated they almost run themselves, with humans monitoring from off-site. A few advanced robotics factories have achieved it—robots building robots. The rest are chasing.

**3. Pilot-to-performance is the chasm that kills**: Many manufacturers struggle scaling beyond initial AI pilots due to insufficient foundational capabilities, organizational readiness gaps, data infrastructure deficits, and talent shortages. Pilots are easy; production is hard.

**4. Five enablers determine success or failure**: Data infrastructure and governance, talent and skills, organizational change management, technology infrastructure, and business model/governance structures. Underinvest in any one, and the transformation stalls.

**5. Build foundations before you scale broadly**: Before scaling AI, establish robust foundational capabilities—assess data readiness, design scalable platforms, develop governance frameworks, create centers of excellence. The sequence matters.

**6. Predictive maintenance is the gateway use case**: Monitor equipment health in real time, predict failures before they occur, reduce unplanned downtime, extend asset life. It's the clearest ROI and the easiest entry point for manufacturing AI.

**7. Production optimization compounds gains**: AI-driven schedule optimization, capacity utilization improvement, waste reduction, and quality enhancement stack on top of each other. The returns compound when workflows connect.

**8. Executive sponsorship separates winners from losers**: Senior leadership commitment, adequate resource allocation, organizational alignment, and cross-functional support determine outcomes. AI transformation without C-suite backing dies in committee.

**9. AI scaling is organizational transformation, not tech implementation**: The companies that win recognize AI scaling as fundamentally about change management, culture, and capability—not just technology deployment. The tech is 20%; the org is 80%.

**10. COOs who invest holistically will own the future**: Strong foundations, organizational change, talent development, governance, and strategic portfolio management—COOs who invest across all fronts will unlock AI's full potential. Half-measures produce half-results.
