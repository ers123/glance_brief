**Bain - State of the Art of Agentic AI Transformation: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. Leaders hit 10-25% EBITDA gains—laggards still pilot**: In 2023-2024, tech-forward enterprises broke through pilots to achieve 10-25% EBITDA gains by scaling AI across core workflows. Most organizations remain stuck in experimentation mode. The leaders aren't waiting.

**2. The playbook exists—stop reinventing it**: Early leaders established a repeatable playbook grounded in robust methodology, analytic tools, and clear benchmarks. Others can follow. The path is mapped; the question is whether you'll take it.

**3. Five critical actions separate winners from losers**: Set ambitious top-down goals (not trials). Charge GMs (not CIO/CTO). Redesign entire workflows (not siloed activities). Curate data as needed (not holistically). Make/buy/partner per workflow (not enterprise-wide).

**4. Process redesign and data cleanup matter most**: The most important aspects of AI transformation are process redesign and cleaning up data and application environments. Technology is secondary. Don't wait for the dust to settle—start the hard work now.

**5. Four levels of agentic AI—most are stuck at Level 1**: Level 1 (info retrieval), Level 2 (single-task agents), Level 3 (cross-system orchestration), Level 4 (multi-agent constellations). Tech-forward companies scaled Level 1 in 2023-24; Levels 2-3 are where capital and deployment converge now.

**6. Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, Salesforce all in**: In H1 2025, major players debuted agentic AI visions. Tech-forward enterprises are already shifting focus from task automation to workflow redesign. The platform war is on.

**7. Agents coexisting safely is the unsolved problem**: Early adopters wrestle with how agents will coexist, discover each other, gather context, and collaborate productively. Add data silos, IP, privacy, security, and vendor lock-in. The vision is clear; reality is messy.

**8. Walled gardens will lead initially**: Expect walled gardens to take the lead, fit-for-purpose custom builds to dominate enterprise architectures, and human-in-the-loop applications to persist for years. Standards battles (MCP, A2A) play out at lightning pace.

**9. Think Iron Man suits, not Iron Man robots**: Plan for human oversight—augmented capabilities, not full autonomy. The practical near-term reality is AI that amplifies human judgment, not replaces it. Set expectations accordingly.

**10. Delay compounds technical debt—act now**: Any further delay in process redesign and data standardization compounds technical debt. The window for catching up is narrowing. Following the taillights of enterprise leaders is the only rational strategy.
