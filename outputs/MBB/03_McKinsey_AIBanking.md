**McKinsey - AI in Banking: Rewiring the Enterprise: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. 40% productivity gains—if you go multiagent**: Banks deploying multiagent AI systems for credit underwriting report 20-60% productivity gains and ~30% faster decision-making. One regional bank hit 40% developer productivity increases. Single models don't move the needle; orchestrated agent teams do.

**2. Labor productivity at US banks is falling**: Despite massive tech spending, labor productivity at US banks is declining. Revenue growth is slowing. Margin pressure is intensifying. AI isn't optional anymore—it's existential for maintaining current ROE levels.

**3. Fewer than 10 domains drive 70-80% of value**: A typical bank has ~25 subdomains that could be transformed, but fewer than 10 drive the vast majority of AI value. Risk, sales, operations, collections, and contact centers top the list. Focus beats spray-and-pray.

**4. AI-first banks treat agents as virtual coworkers**: Leading banks deploy AI agents as virtual employees—capable of planning, reasoning, and using digital tools. These agents handle document collection, assessment, and analysis while humans review outputs and make final calls.

**5. The four-layer stack is non-negotiable**: Winning banks build comprehensive AI stacks: engagement layer (customer experience), decision-making layer (agents and orchestration), data/tech layer (infrastructure), and operating model layer (cross-functional teams). Skip a layer, and the whole thing collapses.

**6. Chatbots and doc summarizers aren't transformation**: Banks achieving material value avoid narrow, siloed projects. They reimagine entire business domains end-to-end rather than deploying isolated chatbots or document summarizers. Incremental automation yields incremental results.

**7. Business executives must own AI—not IT**: The most successful transformations put business executives in the driver's seat, not CIOs or CTOs. When AI remains an IT project, it stays disconnected from value creation. Business ownership changes everything.

**8. The AI control tower tracks value realization**: Top banks establish AI control towers that track value across initiatives, decide what to fund or defund based on results, and ensure capabilities built in one area get reused elsewhere. Governance prevents fragmentation.

**9. 80%+ of developers report improved experience**: Banks that launched gen AI tools for software developers saw 80%+ report improved experience alongside the ~40% productivity boost. Happy developers build better products faster. The talent war just shifted.

**10. Multiagent systems are table stakes by 2026**: Banks will deploy hundreds of trained AI agents across workflows, continuously learning and improving. Humans maintain oversight, but the agents run the processes. The "lights out" bank isn't science fiction—it's the next three years.
