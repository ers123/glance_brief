**BCG x MIT SMR - Leading in the Age of AI Agents: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. 35% already using agentic AI, 44% joining soon**: More than a third of organizations have begun using agentic AI, with another 44% planning to join. That's 79% imminent adoption. The agents have arrived—and they're multiplying fast.

**2. 47% have no AI strategy—and it shows**: Nearly half of respondents indicate they don't have a strategy for what to do with AI. Adoption without direction leads to fragmented efforts and wasted investment. Flying blind into the future.

**3. Agentic AI is half tool, half teammate**: Unlike previous technologies, agentic AI blends tool characteristics (scalable, predictable) with human characteristics (autonomous, adaptive). Traditional management frameworks break down. New playbooks required.

**4. Four tensions define the management challenge**: Scalability vs. adaptability. Investment vs. employment. Supervision vs. autonomy. Process retrofitting vs. process reimagining. Leaders must navigate all four simultaneously—not choose sides.

**5. Two-thirds of leaders will change operating models**: Almost two-thirds of agentic AI leaders expect changes to their operating model. The technology demands organizational reinvention, not just deployment. Structure follows strategy follows capability.

**6. 45% expect fewer middle managers**: Agentic AI leaders anticipate flatter organizations with reduced middle management layers. When AI agents coordinate workflows, traditional spans of control widen. Hierarchy compresses.

**7. 58% will redesign decision rights**: Leading organizations anticipate changes to governance and decision-making authority. Create governance hubs with enterprise-wide guardrails, but treat decision rights as dynamic and workflow-specific.

**8. Dual career paths emerge—orchestrators and specialists**: Organizations need generalist orchestrators (hybrid human-AI team leaders) and AI-augmented specialists (domain experts enhanced by agents). Both tracks are critical for success.

**9. AI agents appreciate with use—unlike every other asset**: Agentic AI systems get smarter and more capable over time. Companies treating them solely as cost-saving tools miss their greater value as engines for scalable learning and innovation.

**10. Strategy beats technology every time**: With the right strategy, imagination, and execution, agentic AI won't just offer promise—it will deliver impact. But only for organizations that recognize its dual nature and align processes, governance, talent, and investments accordingly.
