**KPMG - Global Tech Report 2026: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. 68% aim for top AI maturity by end of 2026, only 24% there today**: Two-thirds of organizations target highest AI maturity by year-end, but only a quarter have achieved it. The ambition-reality gap is massive.

**2. 88% already investing in agentic AI**: Nearly 9 in 10 companies are already investing in agentic AI—autonomous digital agents transforming operations and decision-making. The agentic wave isn't coming; it's here.

**3. 74% report AI creating measurable business value**: Three-quarters see measurable value from AI initiatives—efficiency and risk reduction. But only 24% are scaling AI and achieving ROI across multiple use cases. Value exists; scaling doesn't.

**4. High performers see 4.5x ROI—double the industry average**: Top-performing organizations report 4.5x average ROI from AI, more than double the industry average of 2x. Excellence compounds while mediocrity flatlines.

**5. 53% lack talent for digital transformation**: More than half still lack the talent needed to execute digital transformation plans, even as 90% plan to grow partnerships and ecosystems. The talent bottleneck chokes everything else.

**6. High performers expect half of tech teams to be human by 2027**: Leading organizations anticipate roughly half their tech teams will be permanent human staff by 2027—with AI-augmented ecosystems handling the rest. The hybrid workforce arrives.

**7. 78% agree they must take more risks on emerging tech**: More than three-quarters of organizations agree they must take more risks on emerging technologies to stay relevant. Playing it safe is the riskiest strategy.

**8. Tech debt and cost pressures block AI scaling**: Intensifying challenges of tech debt, cost pressures, and talent shortages hold many back from realizing tech goals. Legacy systems are AI's enemy.

**9. True differentiators: governance, embedding, and execution**: Access to technology or data no longer differentiates. The winners govern AI at scale, embed it into core processes, and execute with discipline company-wide.

**10. 2,500 executives, 27 countries, 8 industries surveyed**: KPMG surveyed 2,500 tech executives across 27 countries and 8 industries. Verdict: the Intelligence Age rewards those who lead, not those who follow.
