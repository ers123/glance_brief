**PwC - 2025 Global AI Jobs Barometer: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. Industries most exposed to AI see 3x productivity growth**: Industries best positioned to adopt AI achieve 3x higher revenue-per-employee growth than industries least able to use AI. The productivity gap is becoming a chasm.

**2. AI-exposed industries: productivity nearly quadrupled since 2022**: Since GenAI's proliferation in 2022, productivity growth nearly quadrupled in AI-exposed industries (financial services, software publishing)—from 7% to 27%. AI isn't incremental; it's exponential.

**3. AI skills command 56% wage premium—up from 25% last year**: Workers with AI skills like prompt engineering command a 56% wage premium, up from 25% last year. The market is pricing AI expertise at a premium that keeps climbing.

**4. Wages rising 2x faster in AI-exposed industries**: Globally, wages are rising more than twice as fast (16.7% vs. 7.9%) in industries most exposed to AI compared to least exposed. AI exposure correlates with prosperity.

**5. Skills changing 66% faster in AI-exposed jobs**: The skills sought by employers are changing 66% faster in AI-exposed occupations—up from 25% last year. The skills earthquake is accelerating, not stabilizing.

**6. Degree requirements falling, especially for AI jobs**: Employer demand for formal degrees is declining everywhere, but especially for AI-exposed jobs. Percentage requiring degrees fell 7-9 points since 2019. Skills beat credentials.

**7. Jobs growing even in highly automatable roles**: Contrary to fears, jobs are growing in virtually every type of AI-exposed occupation, including highly automatable ones. AI is amplifying expertise, enabling employees to multiply impact.

**8. AI jobs growing 7.5% even as total postings fell 11.3%**: Jobs requiring AI skills grew 7.5% year-over-year even as total job postings fell 11.3%. The market is contracting—except for AI talent.

**9. AI impact on women may be unequal**: In every country analyzed, more women than men are in AI-exposed roles, suggesting skills pressure facing women will be higher. Gender equity requires intentional reskilling investment.

**10. AI could boost global GDP 15% over the next decade**: PwC research reveals AI has potential to boost global economic output by up to 15 percentage points over the next decade. $7.1 trillion in revenues set to shift between companies in 2025 alone.
