**Deloitte - State of AI in the Enterprise 2026: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. 25% see transformative AI impact—double from last year**: A quarter of leaders report AI having transformative effect on their companies—more than double the previous year. The inflection point has passed for early movers. Laggards are now visibly behind.

**2. 74% want revenue growth from AI, but only 20% achieve it**: Three-quarters of organizations want AI to grow revenue, but only one in five has seen it happen. The gap between aspiration and execution remains enormous. Wanting isn't getting.

**3. 75% plan agentic AI within two years, only 21% have governance**: Close to three-quarters of companies plan to deploy agentic AI within two years. Yet only 21% report mature agent governance. The race to deploy is outpacing the capacity to control.

**4. Agentic AI market could hit $35-45B by 2030**: Global agentic AI market projected at $8.5B in 2026, potentially reaching $35-45B by 2030—up to 30% higher if enterprises orchestrate agents better. The stakes are multiplying.

**5. 82% expect 10%+ job automation within three years**: The majority expect at least 10% of jobs fully automated within three years. Yet 84% haven't redesigned roles based on AI capabilities. Automation is coming; preparation isn't.

**6. Only 30% redesigning processes around AI**: Less than a third are redesigning key processes around AI; 37% use AI at surface level with minimal workflow change. Sprinkling AI on broken processes yields broken results.

**7. 31% say AI isn't even on the board agenda**: Nearly a third of boards haven't put AI on their agenda—down from 45% but still shocking. Two out of five say AI has changed how they think about board composition. Governance lags reality.

**8. 13% of non-technical workers enthusiastic about AI**: Among non-technical staff, just 13% are highly enthusiastic about AI. 55% are open, but 21% prefer to avoid it and 4% actively distrust it. The human challenge is harder than the technical one.

**9. Mature governance = 28% more AI adoption, 5% higher revenue growth**: Organizations with mature AI governance frameworks see 28% increase in staff using AI and nearly 5% higher revenue growth. Governance isn't overhead—it's accelerator.

**10. 3,235 executives, 24 countries, one verdict—scale or fall behind**: Deloitte surveyed 3,235 business and IT leaders across 24 countries and 6 industries. The conclusion: organizations standing still while others scale will not catch up. The window is closing.
