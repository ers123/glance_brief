**EY - AI in Financial Services 2026: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. 85% of financial services firms now use AI**: As of late 2024, 85% of financial services firms were already using AI—per Bank of England surveys. In 2026, this is essentially universal. The question isn't whether you're using AI; it's how well.

**2. 70%+ of banks use agentic AI, 16% fully deployed**: More than 70% of banking firms report using agentic AI to some degree—16% with fully deployed solutions, 52% running pilots. The agents have infiltrated the industry faster than regulators can track.

**3. 77% say AI will be "very high" importance within two years**: Within two years, 77% of financial services leaders anticipate AI having high or very high overall importance to their businesses. From nice-to-have to must-have in 24 months.

**4. 45% of fintechs offer AI solutions vs. 21% of incumbents**: Nearly half of fintechs offer AI-based B2B solutions compared to just 21% of incumbent banks. The disruptors are out-innovating the established players. David has the better weapons.

**5. Traditional tech companies emerge as finance competitors**: Nearly half of survey respondents cite traditional tech companies as potential major competitors in financial services—enabled by AI. Big Tech could eat banking's lunch.

**6. Data quality and talent are the 80% problem**: Over 80% of respondents cite data quality, data access, and talent as major obstacles for AI implementation. The technology works; the foundations don't.

**7. Agentic AI poses accountability and ethics questions**: EY warns that agentic AI raises fundamental questions about accountability, transparency, and ethics. Who's responsible when the agent makes the call? The legal frameworks don't exist yet.

**8. Four regional regulatory priorities diverge**: US deregulates for innovation; EU simplifies and harmonizes; UK prioritizes growth over risk; APAC emphasizes fintech innovation; LatAm focuses on inclusion. Global firms must navigate fragmented landscapes.

**9. AI adoption outpaces regulatory oversight**: Rapid AI and digital asset advances are outpacing regulatory oversight, prompting new governance models across regions. The rules are being written while the race is being run.

**10. Finance as AI's proving ground**: Finance—data-rich, customer-adopting, and margin-sensitive—will be at the forefront of AI-driven change. Just as steam powered the industrial revolution, AI commoditizes human intelligence. Finance feels it first.
