**OpenAI - State of Enterprise AI 2025: 10 Key Findings (Sharp & Short)**

**1. ChatGPT Enterprise usage up 8x in one year**: Weekly messages in ChatGPT Enterprise increased approximately 8x over the past year. The average worker sends 30% more messages. Adoption isn't slowing—it's accelerating.

**2. Reasoning token consumption up 320x year-over-year**: API reasoning token usage per organization exploded 320x. Enterprises aren't just chatting—they're building serious applications on sophisticated reasoning capabilities.

**3. 1 million+ business customers now using OpenAI tools**: More than one million business customers use OpenAI's products. Enterprise AI crossed from early adopter to mainstream. The market has tipped.

**4. 75% report improved speed or quality**: Three-quarters of workers report AI has improved either speed or quality of output. Daily savings of 40-60 minutes; heavy users report 10+ hours per week. The productivity gains are measured, not claimed.

**5. Frontier firms generate 2x more messages per seat**: Organizations at the AI frontier generate approximately 2x more messages per seat—and 7x more messages to custom GPTs—than median enterprises. The usage gap reveals the capability gap.

**6. Technology, healthcare, manufacturing grow fastest**: Fastest-growing adoption sectors. Professional services, finance, and technology operate at largest scale. Australia, Brazil, Netherlands, France lead geographic growth at 140%+ YoY.

**7. Organizational readiness is the real bottleneck**: The main obstacle to AI adoption is no longer technology—it's organizational readiness. Infrastructure, governance, leadership, and change management determine success. The tech works; the orgs don't.

**8. Custom GPTs separate leaders from followers**: Frontier enterprises create purpose-built AI solutions for specific business needs, embed workflow automations, and integrate deeply with core business systems. Customization is the competitive moat.

**9. Four investments determine AI success**: Infrastructure (technical systems), governance (policies and oversight), leadership (executive vision), and change management (organizational transformation). Underinvest in any, and the whole effort stalls.

**10. The frontier-median gap is widening**: Leaders are pulling further ahead through deeper integration, custom applications, and systematic deployment. Organizational factors matter more than technology access. The rich get richer.
