# The Economic Potential of Generative AI: The Next Productivity Frontier

**Date:** June 14, 2023
**Type:** Report
**Source:** McKinsey & Company - Tech & AI

## Overview

Generative AI is poised to unleash the next wave of productivity. This comprehensive report takes a first look at where business value could accrue and the potential impacts on the workforce.

## Key Insights

- **Economic Value Potential:** Generative AI could add the equivalent of $2.6 trillion to $4.4 trillion in annual value to the global economy
- **75% of Value Concentration:** About 75 percent of the value that generative AI use cases could deliver falls across four main areas: Customer operations, Marketing and sales, Software engineering, and Product R&D
- **Productivity Impact:** Across 16 business functions, researchers examined 63 use cases in which generative AI technology can address specific business problems

## Where Business Value Lies

Generative AI represents a step change in the evolution of artificial intelligence. As companies rush to adopt this technology, understanding where value lies is critical.

### First Lens: Use Case Analysis
- Scans use cases for generative AI that organizations could adopt
- Defines "use cases" as specific business applications where generative AI can deliver significant improvements
- Analysis shows potential to add 15-40% to the $11-17.7 trillion in existing economic value

### Second Lens: Work Activity Impact
- Analyzes generative AI's potential impact on the work activities humans currently perform
- Examines how the technology can augment worker capabilities
- Identifies opportunities for labor productivity improvements

## Industry Impacts

### Banking
- Potential impact: 2.8 to 4.7 percent increase of the industry's annual revenue
- Characteristics positioning banking for AI integration:
  - Sustained digitization efforts with significant technology investments
  - Large customer-facing workforces (call centers, wealth management)
  - Stringent regulatory environment with complex compliance needs
  - White-collar industry with broad employee application potential

**Use Cases in Banking:**
- Virtual expert chatbots augmenting employee performance using proprietary knowledge
- Environmental, social, and governance (ESG) analysis and reporting
- Back-office cost reduction through automated processing
- Code acceleration for software development and tech debt reduction
- Tailored content production at scale

### Retail and Consumer Packaged Goods
- Technology can create significant value through:
  - Reinvention of customer interaction patterns
  - Accelerated creation of value in key marketing and sales areas
  - Rapid resolution and enhanced insights in customer care
  - Disruptive and creative innovation in product development

**Key Considerations:**
- Understanding whether generated content is based on fact or inference
- Managing adversarial attacks on foundation models
- Keeping humans in the loop for quality assurance

### Pharmaceuticals and Medical Products
- Significant potential across the entire value chain
- Impact areas:
  - Drug discovery and lead identification
  - Indication finding for new drug uses
  - Clinical trial optimization

**Important Factors:**
- Need for human-in-the-loop quality checks
- Explainability of AI-generated recommendations
- Privacy considerations with clinical data and medical records

## Business Function Applications

### Customer Operations: Improving Customer and Agent Experiences
- **Customer Self-Service:** Generative AI chatbots provide immediate, personalized responses to complex inquiries
- **Resolution During Initial Contact:** Instant data retrieval about customers to enable first-contact resolution
- **Reduced Response Time:** AI can cut customer service representative response time significantly
- **Increased Sales:** Rapid processing of customer data and browsing history enables cross-selling and upselling

**Productivity Impact:** Estimated productivity increase of 30-45% in customer care functions

### Marketing and Sales: Boosting Personalization, Content Creation, and Sales Productivity

**Marketing Benefits:**
- Efficient and effective content creation
- Enhanced use of unstructured, inconsistent data
- SEO optimization through AI-assisted search strategies
- Personalized product discovery and search

**Sales Benefits:**
- Lead identification and prioritization with comprehensive consumer profiles
- Improved lead development through synthesized sales information
- Estimated productivity increase of approximately 15-25% in sales functions

### Software Engineering: Speeding Developer Work as a Coding Assistant
- Treats computer languages as just another language for AI processing
- Demonstrates significant potential based on GitHub Copilot research
- Direct productivity impact: Potential for substantial improvement
- Long-term benefits include improved application quality and reduced development time

### Product R&D: Reducing Research and Design Time, Improving Simulation and Testing
- Life sciences and chemical industries are already using generative AI foundation models
- Benefit areas:
  - **Enhanced Design:** More efficient material selection and cost reduction
  - **Improved Product Testing and Quality:** Higher-quality products through generative design
  - **Deep Learning Surrogates:** New applications for complex simulation tasks

## Work and Productivity Implications

### Automation Potential

Experts assess that technology could achieve human-level performance on an expanding range of work activities:
- Significant acceleration of automation potential compared to previous assessments
- Compressed timeline between early and late adoption scenarios
- Faster development cycles than earlier generations of automation technology

### Adoption Scenarios

Adoption timelines account for:
- Time required to integrate technological capabilities
- Technical feasibility assessment
- Economic justification for deployment
- Organizational change management

**Key Findings:**
- Adoption likely to be faster in developed countries where wages are higher
- Varies significantly based on regulatory environment and organizational readiness

### Generative AI's Impact on Knowledge Work

- Biggest impact expected on knowledge work, particularly:
  - Collaboration activities
  - Application of expertise
  - Communication-focused work
  - Supervisory activities
  - Documentation tasks

**Occupation Impact:**
- Potential for technical automation is highest in occupations requiring higher education
- Historical contrast: Previous automation had most impact on lower-wage occupations
- Generative AI reversal: Expected to most transform higher-wage knowledge worker roles

### Productivity Growth Implications

- Global economic growth slowed from 2012-2022 compared to previous decades
- Declining employment growth compounds economic challenges
- Generative AI could enable labor productivity growth of 0.1 to 0.6 percent annually through 2040
- Productivity growth is the main engine of GDP growth

**Conditions for Success:**
- Effective redeployment of labor hours
- Workforce reskilling and adaptation
- Organizational change management
- Policy support for workforce transitions

## Considerations for Business and Society

### Using Generative AI Responsibly

Organizations must address various risks from the start:

**Fairness:** Models may generate algorithmic bias due to imperfect training data or engineering decisions

**Intellectual Property:** Training data and outputs can create IP risks, including copyright infringement concerns

**Privacy:** User input information could appear in model outputs in unauthorized ways

**Security:** Bad actors may use generative AI to accelerate cyberattacks

**Explainability:** Neural networks with billions of parameters challenge our understanding of how models work

**Reliability:** Inconsistent answers to the same prompts impede assessment and trust

**Organizational Impact:** Technology significantly affects workforce, with varied impacts across groups and communities

**Social and Environmental Impact:** Development and training of foundation models may have detrimental social and environmental consequences

### Key Questions for Different Stakeholders

**Companies and Business Leaders:**
- How to capture value quickly while managing risks?
- How will required occupations and skills transform?
- What role should companies play in preventing negative use cases?
- How to transparently share scaling experiences?

**Policy Makers:**
- What will the future of work look like economically?
- How can workers be supported through activity shifts?
- What retraining programs should be prioritized?
- How to prevent harmful uses of generative AI?
- How to ensure human-centric AI development?

**Individuals (Workers, Consumers, Citizens):**
- What level of concern is appropriate?
- How to balance convenience with privacy and risks?
- Can citizens have a voice in deployment decisions?

## Conclusion

All stakeholders are at the beginning of a journey to understand generative AI's power, reach, and capabilities. The technology has potential to create enormous value for the global economy at a time when productivity growth is crucial. However, responsible deployment and thoughtful consideration of societal impacts are essential for realizing this potential while minimizing risks.

The era of generative AI is just beginning, with both significant opportunities and important challenges ahead for businesses, policymakers, and society.
