## Executive Summary

To gain material value from AI, banks need to move beyond experimentation to transform critical business areas, including by reimagining complex workflows with multiagent systems. While initial experimentation has occurred, many banks face challenges in moving from proof of concept to proof of value and realizing tangible returns on AI investments.

---

## The Challenge for Banks

- **Uneven productivity results:** Labor productivity falling at US banks despite high tech spending
- **Slowing growth:** Facing revenue and loan growth slowdown
- **Increased competition:** Competing with fintechs, neobanks, payment solutions, private credit firms
- **Margin pressure:** Need to cut costs faster as revenue growth slows to maintain current ROE

---

## What AI-First Banks Do Differently

Leading banks that excel in AI demonstrate four key practices:

### 1. Set a Bold, Bankwide Vision for Value
- View AI not just as cost reduction, but as revenue enhancement and customer/employee experience driver
- Create expansive outlook on AI's transformational role
- Embed AI in strategic planning across all business units
- Focus on high-impact, core strategic areas (not peripheral safe bets)

### 2. Root Transformation in Business Value
- Transform entire business domains and subdomains, not just isolated use cases
- Avoid narrow, siloed projects (chatbots, document summarizers in isolation)
- Reimagine end-to-end workflows and processes
- Ensure business executives take ownership and lead design

### 3. Build Comprehensive AI Stack Powered by Multiagent Systems
- Implement four-layer capability stack:
  - **Engagement:** Reimagine customer interactions
  - **Decision-making:** AI agents and orchestration
  - **Data and core tech:** Infrastructure and data pipelines
  - **Operating model:** Cross-functional teams and AI control tower
- Move beyond traditional analytics to multiagent systems

### 4. Sustain and Scale Value
- Set up critical enablers including cross-functional teams and AI control tower
- Establish governance and risk guardrails
- Drive reusability of AI capabilities across enterprise
- Ensure continuous learning and improvement

## What Does an AI-First Bank Look Like?

Essential elements include:

1. **Customer Experience Reimagining**
   - Personalized offers and frictionless experiences
   - Seamless across devices and partner ecosystems

2. **AI-Driven Decision Making**
   - Real-time analytical insights
   - Precise, customer-need-specific messaging

3. **Modern Technology Stack**
   - Automated cloud provisioning

   - Continuous, secure data integration

4. **Platform Operating Model**

## Real-World Examples

### Large Bank: Enterprise-Wide AI
- Using AI across enterprise for customer and employee experience
- **Retail banking:** Personalized investment/financial planning nudges
- **Small business:** AI identifies at-risk loans, enabling intervention
- **Results:** Enhanced efficiency, revenue, and profitability

### Regional Bank: Developer Productivity
- Launched gen AI tools for software developers
- **Results:** ~40% productivity increase, 80%+ of developers report improved experience

## Choosing the Right Domains for Transformation

### Domain Selection Strategy
A typical bank has ~25 subdomains that could be transformed:
- **Fewer than 10 subdomains** should be first transformation candidates
- These can drive **70-80% of total incremental value**
- Examples: Risk, sales, operations, relationship management, collections, contact-center servicing

### Evaluation Criteria

**Business Impact Assessment:**
- Value quantifiability
- Strategic alignment
- User adoption readiness
- Business priority alignment

**Technical Feasibility Assessment:**
- Data availability and quality
- Handling of sensitive data
- Scaling techniques
- Component reusability
- Legacy system compatibility

## The AI Bank Stack: Four Capability Layers

### Engagement Layer
- Reimagine customer engagement
- Intelligent, personalized, frictionless experiences
- Human-like conversational AI via text/voice
- Seamless omnichannel experience (mobile, web, branches, contact centers)

### Decision-Making Layer (The Brain)
- **Traditional analytics:** Models and analytics modules
- **Agent and AI orchestration:** New elements for complex decision-making
- Orchestrates thousands of AI-powered decisions
- Affects both customers (product recommendations) and employees (credit approval, fraud detection)

### Core Technology and Data Layer
- **Reusable tools and pipelines:** ML operations for large language models at scale
- **Enterprise data infrastructure:** Storage and access for unstructured data
- **API architecture:** Modern, secure data exchange
- **Cybersecurity:** Robust protection

### Operating Model
- **Cross-functional platform teams:** Break organizational silos
- **Agility and speed:** Faster decision-making and execution
- **Aligned goals:** Better priority alignment across enterprise
- **AI control tower:** Tracks value realization, governance, adoption

## Orchestrated Multiagent Systems: A Game-Changer

### What Are Multiagent Systems?
- Virtual coworkers enabled by gen AI advances
- Capable of planning, reasoning, and acting using digital tools
- Still nascent but showing promise for transformative impact
- Expected to improve continuously as technology develops

### Two-Layer AI Architecture

**AI Orchestration Layer:**
- Handles complex workflows and task planning
- Autonomously plans actions, reaches decisions
- Uses existing tools, in-house data, other agents
- Manifests as copilots for employees/customers
- Example: Credit manager copilot orchestrating entire loan workflow
- Requires human oversight for now; will become more autonomous

**AI Agent Layer:**
- Completes specialized tasks as instructed
- Fine-tuned with domain-specific data and human feedback
- Examples:
  - Policy agent: Suggests appropriate loan terms
  - Collateral inspection agent: Screens documents/images for fraud
  - Computer vision: Detects doctored photos

### AI Enablers
- Reusable components and services (retrieval, intent detection, summarization)
- Safety elements (data confidentiality, enterprise risk guardrails)
- Speed and safe deployment

### Business Impact Example: Credit Underwriting

**Traditional workflow:** Humans handle every step (document collection, customer discussion, collateral assessment, offer presentation)

**With multiagent systems:**
- AI agents: Document collection, assessment, analysis
- Human credit manager: Reviews outputs, customer interaction, collateral inspection, final offer
- **Results:** 20-60% productivity gains (varies by factors), ~30% faster decision-making

### Future Potential
- Banks could have hundreds of trained AI agents
- Continuously learn and improve
- Embedded across workflows
- Humans maintain oversight and auditing capability

## Building the AI Transformation Foundation

### Critical Sublayers to Invest In

**Industrial AI/Machine Learning Sublayer:**
- Reusable tools and pipelines
- Full ML operations capabilities
- Cost-effective LLM deployment and scaling

**Enterprise Data Sublayer:**
- Storage, transformation, and access for unstructured data
- Critical for training multiagent systems
- Large dataset infrastructure

## The Transformation Path: From Pilot to Scale

1. **Start with one subdomain**
2. **Develop multiple use cases** within that subdomain
3. **Progress through phases:** MVP → more sophisticated stages
4. **Create reusable components** from first subdomain
5. **Apply to other subdomains** systematically

### Building the Stack in Phases
- Don't try to create everything at once
- Build and improve AI stack iteratively
- Leverage learnings from early phases for later implementations

---

## Making the Transformation Stick

### Creating Value, Not Just Models
- Cross-functional teams blending banking and technical expertise
- Adequate stakeholder representation
- Aligned incentives across teams
- Embed risk management from the beginning
- Focus on deployment and adoption, not just technical build

### Sustaining Value: The AI Control Tower

**Three key roles:**

1. **Custodian of Bankwide AI Strategy**
   - Track value realization across initiatives
   - Decide which areas to fund/defund based on results
   - Continuously refine comprehensive AI roadmap

2. **Coordinator of Enterprise Decisions**
   - Design choices for tools and architecture
   - Establish central standards and risk protocols
   - Ensure cohesion across business units

3. **Driver of Asset Reusability**
   - Connect teams across organization
   - Accelerate best practice dissemination
   - Ensure AI capabilities built in one area reused elsewhere
   - Reduce duplicative effort, speed time-to-market

---

## Rewiring the Enterprise Checklist

Banks assessing AI transformation readiness should evaluate:

- [ ] Comprehensive, bankwide AI vision with ROI measurement (vs. siloed dabbling)
- [ ] Full-stack approach (gen AI + analytics AI + digital tools together)
- [ ] Entire domain reimagining (vs. narrow isolated use cases)
- [ ] Multiagent systems for complex workflows (vs. single models)
- [ ] Component reusability and coordination (vs. building from scratch each project)

---

## Six Critical Enterprise Capabilities for AI Success

(From McKinsey's broader Rewired research, based on 200+ at-scale AI transformations)

1. **Business-led digital roadmap**
2. **Right talent with required skills**
3. **Fit-for-purpose operating model**
4. **Easy-to-use technology**
5. **Continually enriched, accessible data**
6. **Adoption and scaling of digital solutions**

All elements are interconnected; underinvestment in any single area can sabotage the entire transformation.
