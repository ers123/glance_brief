# Convergence: Human + AI for the Next Era of Finance

## Executive Overview

The world is being shaped by the rapid rise of artificial intelligence. What was once hype has now evolved into an **AI super-cycle**, with unprecedented levels of investment and adoption transforming both competitiveness and innovation.

Institutions are moving from **pilots and proofs of concept to enterprise-scale deployments**, shifting the focus from experimentation to tangible value creation—driving efficiency, inclusion, and new revenue streams.

At the same time, leaders must navigate talent gaps, data, and infrastructure challenges, while ensuring that governance, responsibility, and resilience remain at the core of AI adoption.

## Report Structure

This flagship report from BCG, developed as the thought leadership partner of the Global Fintech Fest 2025 (GFF), is organized into four chapters:

### Chapter 1: The Human + AI Frontier: Global and Local

Situates the AI super-cycle alongside past technological inflection points. The chapter illustrates how AI can transform everyday life and business operations through conceptual applications.

**Key Examples:**

**JanArth.AI** - Personal Finance Management App
- Current pricing: ₹150–250/month
- Potential future pricing: ₹50/month (within 3–4 years)
- Shows how AI can democratize financial services

**VyapaeSaathi.AI** - CFO-Like Companion for MSMEs
- Brings sophisticated financial management to micro, small, and medium enterprises
- Demonstrates AI's inclusive potential

**Key Insight:** These examples highlight the **inclusive potential of AI** when built on India's population-scale digital public infrastructure.

### Chapter 2: AI for India: Opportunity and Imperatives

Explores how India can leverage its **trifecta advantage**:
1. World's largest AI consumer market
2. Leading use-case playground
3. Global execution hub

**Key Imperatives Across the AI Stack:**
- Scaling infrastructure and compute
- Expanding ethical data commons (e.g., AIKosh)
- Creating domain-specific models
- Unlocking ₹10 lakh crore of patient capital
- Investing in AI talent
- Embedding AI applications at scale

**Revenue Potential:** India can generate **$100–120 billion in AI software & services revenue by 2028**

### Chapter 3: Finance.AI and the Voice of Industry

Captures perspectives of BFSI leaders, founders, policymakers, and investors.

**Current AI Performance:**
- Leading financial institutions achieving **20–25% cost efficiencies**
- **10–20% revenue uplift** through AI
- **However**, only **27% of banks are future-ready**

**10-Point Blueprint for BFSI to Realize AI Benefits at Scale:**

Covers strategy, talent, infrastructure, and execution across five key areas:
1. Strategic alignment and governance
2. Talent development and acquisition
3. Infrastructure and technology
4. Data quality and management
5. Execution and scaling

**Industry Challenges:**
- Lack of coherent strategies
- Gaps in talent, data, and infrastructure
- Need to scale beyond pilots
- Importance of adopting UPI-style open innovation to unlock AI's full potential

**Key Finding:** AI brought **innovation back to the top of the CXO agenda**, yet challenges persist

### Chapter 4: Global Fintech Trends

Provides an update on global and Indian fintech funding, revenues, and segment-wise developments.

**Key Insights:**
- **APAC and NAMR** will be the future centers of fintech gravity
- **India continues to play a pivotal role** in shaping the trajectory of global financial services
- Global fintech ecosystem showing sustained growth and regional shifts

## Key Themes

### The AI Super-Cycle

The convergence of human expertise with AI capabilities is creating unprecedented value:
- **Cost Efficiency:** 20–25% improvements in banking operations
- **Revenue Growth:** 10–20% uplift through new business models
- **Innovation Acceleration:** Bringing strategic initiatives to board level

### Inclusive Growth

AI's potential extends beyond financial institutions to:
- Individual consumers (fintech apps for personal finance)
- Micro and small enterprises (AI-powered financial management)
- Emerging markets (scalable, affordable solutions)

### Infrastructure and Talent

Success requires investments in:
- Cloud infrastructure and compute capacity
- Ethical AI frameworks and data governance
- Skilled workforce (AI/ML engineers, domain experts)
- Domain-specific AI models

## India's Strategic Position

India's advantages position it to become a global AI powerhouse:
1. **Massive Consumer Base:** Largest potential market for AI applications
2. **Innovation Labs:** Real-world testing grounds for AI use cases
3. **Tech Talent:** Global execution capabilities and engineering expertise
4. **Digital Infrastructure:** UPI and digital payment systems as foundation

## Challenges and Solutions

**Challenges:**
- Fragmented strategies across institutions
- Talent scarcity in AI/ML specialties
- Data quality and privacy concerns
- Regulatory uncertainty

**Solutions:**
- Unified industry frameworks (like the 10-point blueprint)
- Investment in talent development and upskilling
- Ethical data commons (AIKosh model)
- Collaborative approach with regulators

## Forward-Looking Perspective

The financial services industry is at an inflection point. Organizations that successfully navigate the convergence of human expertise and AI capabilities will:
- Drive enterprise-wide transformation
- Unlock new revenue streams
- Improve operational efficiency
- Create more inclusive financial services
- Shape the future of global finance
