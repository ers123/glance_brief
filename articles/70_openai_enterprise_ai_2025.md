# The State of Enterprise AI 2025 Report

**URL:** https://openai.com/index/the-state-of-enterprise-ai-2025-report/

**Publisher:** OpenAI

**Published:** December 2025

**Report Type:** Enterprise Adoption & Usage Analysis

## Executive Summary

OpenAI released the State of Enterprise AI 2025 report, providing a comprehensive analysis of how enterprises are adopting AI, what workers are gaining, and how organizational leaders are turning experimentation into measurable productivity and new capabilities. The report is based on de-identified, aggregated usage data from enterprise customers and a survey of 9,000 workers across nearly 100 enterprises.

## Key Growth Metrics

### ChatGPT Enterprise Usage
- **Weekly Messages Growth:** Weekly messages in ChatGPT Enterprise increased approximately 8x over the past year
- **Average Worker Engagement:** The average worker is sending 30% more messages
- **API Growth:** Reasoning token consumption per organization increased 320x year-over-year

### Market Penetration
- **Business Customers:** More than 1 million business customers now use OpenAI's tools
- **Adoption Acceleration:** Significant growth across enterprise segments

## Productivity Gains

### Worker-Reported Improvements
- **75% of workers** report that using AI at work has improved either the speed or quality of their output
- **Daily Time Savings:** Workers report saving 40-60 minutes per day with AI assistance
- **Heavy User Savings:** Heavy users report more than 10 hours per week in time savings
- **Measurable Outcomes:** Conversion of experimentation into demonstrated productivity gains

## The Adoption Divide: Frontier vs. Median Enterprises

### Frontier Firms Performance
- Generate **approximately 2x** more messages per seat than median enterprises
- Generate **7x more messages** to custom GPTs than median enterprises
- Demonstrate markedly deeper organizational integration
- Systematically embed AI into organizational operations

### Integration Characteristics of Frontier Firms
- **Custom Applications:** Purpose-built AI solutions for specific business needs
- **Workflow Automations:** Systematic automation of business processes
- **Core Business Integration:** Deep integration with existing business systems and infrastructure
- **Organizational Readiness:** Investment in infrastructure, governance, leadership, and change management

## Organizational Success Factors

### The Challenge: Organizational Readiness, Not Technology
The report emphasizes a critical insight: the main obstacle to AI adoption is **no longer technology**, but **organizational readiness**.

### Key Success Factors
Successful companies investing in:
1. **Infrastructure** - Technical systems and platforms to support AI
2. **Governance** - Clear policies, guidelines, and oversight structures
3. **Leadership** - Executive commitment and strategic vision for AI adoption
4. **Change Management** - Systematic approach to organizational transformation

## Adoption Patterns Across Enterprises

## Implementation Lessons

### From Experimentation to Productivity
The report documents how organizations are moving beyond early-stage AI experiments to:
- Measurable productivity improvements
- Integrated workflows
- Systematic deployment across teams
- Organizational-wide transformation

### The Frontier-Median Gap
The significant difference between frontier and median enterprises suggests:
- Adoption is not evenly distributed
- Leaders are pulling further ahead in AI integration
- Organizational factors matter more than technological access
- Success requires multi-faceted investment beyond just tools

## Implications for Enterprise Leaders

1. **Technology Readiness:** AI tools are ready and available
2. **Organizational Gap:** Most organizations lag in governance, infrastructure, and change management
3. **Competitive Advantage:** Early movers with strong organizational readiness are gaining significant advantages
4. **Investment Priority:** Focus on organizational capabilities alongside technology deployment
5. **Geographic Opportunities:** Emerging markets showing rapid growth rates

## Conclusions

The State of Enterprise AI 2025 report reveals:

1. **Rapid Adoption:** Enterprise AI adoption is accelerating dramatically with 8x growth in engagement
2. **Proven Value:** Workers are experiencing measurable productivity gains (40-60 min/day)
3. **Organizational Reality:** Success depends on organizational infrastructure, governance, and change management rather than technology availability
4. **Competitive Dynamics:** Frontier firms are establishing significant competitive advantages through deeper AI integration
5. **Global Expansion:** Regional growth variation suggests AI adoption is following patterns of technological leadership and organizational readiness

The report positions organizational readiness and systematic integration as the key differentiators between enterprises that succeed with AI and those that lag.
