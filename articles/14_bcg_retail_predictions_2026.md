# 2026 Retail Predictions

## Introduction

The retail landscape is transforming, as automation and AI unlock new possibilities for businesses and the consumers they serve. While the implications vary by geography, sector, and a multitude of other factors, giving retailers across the globe different views of the future, the overall picture is one of **fundamental change**.

Based on BCG's analysis of the forces, trends, and challenges impacting retailers and consumers, we have developed six predictions for the year ahead.

## Key Context

The retail industry faces unprecedented transformation driven by:
- **Automation**: Streamlining operations and customer experiences
- **Artificial Intelligence**: Creating new business models and consumer value propositions
- **Global Variation**: Different impacts across geographies and sectors

## Industry Impact

The convergence of automation and AI is reshaping:
- **Consumer Expectations**: New standards for personalization and convenience
- **Business Models**: Shift from traditional retail to omnichannel and AI-driven approaches
- **Competitive Dynamics**: Speed and innovation become critical differentiators
- **Operational Efficiency**: Automation reduces costs while improving service quality

## Implications for Retailers

Retailers across all sectors and geographies must:
1. Embrace technological transformation
2. Understand region-specific and sector-specific implications
3. Plan for fundamental shifts in consumer behavior
4. Prepare organizational capabilities for AI and automation adoption
