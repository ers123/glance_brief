# How AI Agents and Tech Will Transform Healthcare in 2026

## Industry Overview

As 2026 unfolds, health care organizations are embracing artificial intelligence technology to an unprecedented degree across a wide range of activities, from patient care to clinical workflows to drug discovery and development. AI's potential to improve patient outcomes, enhance efficiency, and dramatically accelerate the pace of innovation is redefining how health care creates value.

From its start as a rules-based machine learning technology, AI has evolved and, through GenAI, acquired the ability to follow instructions that generate new content and new insights. Now, with the emergence of AI agents that can autonomously plan and execute tasks with minimal human oversight, we're seeing an explosion of opportunities in health care. As the technology becomes increasingly integrated into health care ecosystems, we expect the market for AI in health care to expand rapidly.

## The 10-20-70 Rule

Change of this magnitude requires critical adjustments on many fronts. Successful AI innovators follow the 10-20-70 rule:

- **10%** - effort to algorithms
- **20%** - to technology and data
- **70%** - to people and processes

This emphasis is crucial because change management is difficult to get right—and successful transformation depends on people. As health care roles and work evolve, AI agents should enhance and augment the human workforce.

**Organizations should start planning now for the workforce they will need in five years**, including upskilling current workers and redesigning their roles to meet the requirements of evolving organizational structures. How organizations manage these changes will drive their impact on value.

## Three Key Transformation Areas

### 1. Patients Now Occupy the Driver's Seat

Patients are exercising more control over their health than ever before, connecting directly with their providers through patient portals and using digital tools to track and monitor their health.

**Key Statistics:**
- Close to half of US adults use health apps
- About a third use wearable devices that keep tabs on their health metrics

**Expected Developments:**
- Providers will use AI tools to analyze data from personal devices, genetic information, and diagnosis/treatment details from electronic medical records
- AI will predict health problems before they start and prescribe personalized solutions
- Pharmaceutical and medical device companies are prioritizing direct-to-patient relationships
- Treatments will be placed directly in consumers' hands, sidestepping traditional intermediaries like pharmacies and insurers

**Digital and AI Tools:**
- Holistic and wellness solutions will integrate data from multiple sources
- These tools will guide people in living longer and optimizing cognitive health
- Focus on sleep, nutrition, exercise, and mindfulness

### 2. Providers' Toolbox Expands

**Ambient AI Scribes:**
- Record and summarize patient conversations
- Reduce time physicians spend documenting interactions, drafting notes, and responding to messages
- Free physicians to focus more time on patient care

**AI Clinical Assistants/Co-Pilots:**
- Instantaneously synthesize patient data, symptoms, and latest research
- Improve clinician productivity
- Reduce diagnostic errors

**Precision Medicine:**
- AI-supported precision medicine tailored to individual genetics, environment, and lifestyle
- Enable providers to predict Alzheimer's or kidney disease years before symptoms appear
- Targeted drugs and precision imaging enable one-step cancer diagnosis and treatment
- Moving into mainstream care

**Agentic AI in Drug Development:**
- Will compress the timeline for new drug development from years to months
- Generate new molecules and simulate how they will interact and behave in the body

### 3. Health Care Ecosystems Recalibrate

AI is fundamentally changing every aspect of health care, from R&D to direct patient care. Its accelerating adoption has profound implications for how health care players conceive of and carry out their roles.

**Strategic Focus:**
- Successful organizations concentrate on a small number of opportunities with transformative potential, rather than implementing dozens of AI pilots
- Key areas: precision medicine, clinical workflow automation, and personalized care

**Human-Centered Approach:**
- As AI improves productivity and reduces overhead, ensure a human-centered approach
- Focus on training employees to work alongside their intelligent partners
- Embed AI literacy in professional development

**Quality Assurance:**
- As the adoption of powerful AI tools expands, prioritize rigorous quality assurance to safeguard reliability
- Evaluation and testing are essential

## Looking Forward

As AI and digital continue to transform health care in 2026, the industry faces an exciting but demanding landscape. Organizations that balance technological innovation with human-centered change management will be best positioned to create value and improve outcomes.
